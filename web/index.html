THE PROBLEM

Paying people is easy.
Knowing who was paid is harder.

↗

Separate transfers, scattered records

One contractor payment in a wallet, another in a spreadsheet, another screenshot in a chat. Reconciling them is needlessly manual.

⧉

Duplicate payments are expensive mistakes

When a transaction looks stuck, resending can create a second payment. Setlora's contract tracks invoice numbers for each payer.

◎

“Sent” doesn't always mean settled

A broadcast hash alone isn't proof. Setlora distinguishes pending, failed, mismatched, and verified settlement evidence.
