### How to verify

1. Open the public payment verifier.
2. Paste a complete transaction hash: `0x` followed by **64 hexadecimal characters**.
3. Click **Verify payment**.
4. Inspect the contract, payer, block, batch total, invoice amounts, recipients, and confirmation summary.
5. Copy the shareable verification link if the result is verified.

Use the documented [three-recipient example](/docs/how-to-test/) if you do not have your own transaction.

### How to interpret the result

- **Verified:** on-chain receipt, invoice events, batch total and payer-specific settlement flags match the expected Setlora contract.
- **Not verified / mismatch:** required evidence is missing or inconsistent. Do not present the payment as complete.
- **Unavailable / unknown:** the chain or RPC could not provide enough information. An outage is not evidence that the payment failed; do not blindly resend it.

<Aside type="caution" title="A screenshot is not settlement proof">
Only on-chain evidence can establish the testnet payment status. A browser-local payment history entry, approved token allowance or unverified transaction hash is not enough.
</Aside>

Public verification does **not** need a connected wallet and never sends a transaction.
