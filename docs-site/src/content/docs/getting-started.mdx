### What Setlora does

Setlora lets a payer prepare several invoice-linked transfers in one **atomic** batch. If the smart-contract transaction fails, none of its transfers should remain completed from that transaction. Successful payments are checked against blockchain receipts, invoice events, batch totals, and settlement flags.

A normal journey is: **Prepare invoices → Review and check recipients → Approve test pUSD (if needed) → Confirm the batch in your wallet → Verify evidence.**

### What you need

- **For browsing or public verification:** no wallet or tokens.
- **For making a test payment:** an EVM-compatible browser wallet connected to Portaldot V3 Testnet, test pUSD, and testnet gas funds.
- **For recipients in the current payment form:** valid EVM-format `0x...` addresses. Native SS58 addresses are not accepted for EVM batch settlement.

Setlora is **non-custodial**. It does not collect your seed phrase or sign transactions for you. A token approval alone does not transfer money.

### A first test without spending

1. Open [Send payments](https://setlora.pages.dev/app) and enter a fresh invoice number, a valid EVM address and a positive pUSD amount.
2. Click **Review batch**. This is a read-only check; it does not send a payment.
3. Examine the invoice breakdown, recipient status, total, balance and allowance.
4. Stop before **Approve** or **Confirm batch settlement** unless you explicitly intend to transact with test tokens.

For an even faster judge walkthrough, use [How to test](/docs/how-to-test/).
