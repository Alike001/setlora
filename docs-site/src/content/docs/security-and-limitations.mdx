---
title: Security and limitations
description: Important boundaries for Setlora's current testnet release.
---

### What Setlora does verify

- The payer signs using their own EVM wallet; Setlora does not hold signing keys.
- Token approval and settlement are **separate** wallet confirmations.
- The batch contract is designed for **all-or-nothing EVM transfers** in a single transaction.
- Invoice IDs are protected against repeated settlement for the same payer and contract.
- Confirmation requires the expected receipt, invoice events, batch total, and on-chain invoice flags.
- Unknown or incomplete chain evidence must remain unknown rather than being reported as success.

### What Setlora does not do yet

- Mainnet or real-money production payments
- Independent security audit
- Native SS58 payouts or sponsored account activation
- Cross-chain settlement
- Automatic history synchronization between browsers/devices
- Guaranteed account readiness from a single native storage lookup

### Privacy and records

Invoice numbers and payment events are visible on-chain in the testnet route. Browser-local history is convenience data, not independent proof. Avoid including personal recipient details in shared screenshots or public bug reports.

**Setlora is experimental Portaldot V3 Testnet software. Never treat it as an audited financial service.**
