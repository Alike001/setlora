# Setlora — Brand Foundation

Name: Setlora

Tagline: One batch. Every payment accounted for.

Category: Onchain business payment operations

Initial network: Portaldot V3

Primary audience:
Teams, DAOs, businesses, and organizations
paying multiple contributors or suppliers.

Core promise:
Prepare, authorize, execute, and reconcile
multiple payments with verifiable records.

Visual identity:
- Midnight Ink: #0B1724
- Settlement Mint: #19BC9A
- Cloud White: #F5F8F7
- Pending Amber: #F0B45C

Product principles:
- Self-custody
- Explicit payment authorization
- Accurate transaction status
- Invoice-aware duplicate protection
- Verifiable payment records
- Honest failure and recovery states
